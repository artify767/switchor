# Switchor® — Editorial Industrial Luxury Website

An 8-page story-driven, high-engagement commercial website built with a Warm Architectural Stone × Machined Obsidian × Copper & Cobalt visual philosophy.

## Highlights
- **Architectural Weight-Contrast Typography**: Powered by `Plus Jakarta Sans` ExtraLight/Light (`200–300`) contrasted with upright Bold (`700`) focal accents (`One grade. Mastered to the micron.`) across all 8 pages.
- **Full-Bleed Captivating Hero Stage**: Edge-to-edge obsidian stage with dual selectable macro photography (`Live Shear` & `Sintered Form`), live YG6 metallurgical telemetry, interactive 5-profile quick selector, and direct 3D packaging inspector launch.
- **Interactive Crisp Vector 3D Packaging Showcase**: Built directly into the mid-page dark studio showcase — drag to rotate 360°, switch across all 5 YG6 models (A416, A420, A425, C122, C125), inspect live Code 128 barcodes & EU GHS08 safety panels, and click any of the 4 packaging innovation cards to rotate the box smoothly to that exact detail.
- **Official UAE Dirham (AED) Currency Symbol**: Built-in scalable vector UAE Dirham symbol (Central Bank of the UAE 2025 design) paired with live pegged AED / USD profit calculator.
- **Interactive "Tear & Pin the Reorder Stub" Lab**: Visitors literally tear the perforated top strip off a Switchor box and watch it pin onto a stock board.
- **Live Interactive Google Map Finale**: Built into the final section of `index.html` and `contact.html` with interactive hub switching (Dubai UAE, Stuttgart DE, Zhuzhou CN) and one-click Google Maps directions.
- **8 Complete Pages**: `index.html`, `collection.html`, `partners.html`, `craft.html`, `workshop.html`, `roadmap.html`, `contact.html`, `legal.html`.

## Deploy
Unzip and drag `switchor-editorial/` onto Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or any static web server, or open `index.html` locally.
